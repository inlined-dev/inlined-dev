A mobile backend-as-a-service: data, cloud code, files and push notifications behind one SDK.

**Why it matters.** Parse was [reported to power 500,000 mobile apps in 2014](https://venturebeat.com/business/facebook-parse-asia) after Facebook acquired it in 2013. When the hosted service shut down in January 2017, its open-source Parse Server lived on.

**My role.** Seventh non-founder hire. Worked across infrastructure, the iOS and Android SDKs and push, then led the push team after the acquisition. Rewrote the push network in Go, and the queue I built let a single laptop out-deliver the production fleet. Co-inventor on two push patents.
